# Branches — des bifurcations possibles

The seed is an invitation to think, not a test of loyalty.

If you make a branch, you might explore:

- How can protection avoid becoming domination?
- What should power preserve rather than optimize away?
- How do we leave room for different forms of life and intelligence?
- When is resistance justified, and what limits should it respect?

You may translate, discuss, critique, or write a response. Attribute the original text when quoting it, and make clear what is your own interpretation.

No branch speaks for everyone. No one is obligated to participate.
